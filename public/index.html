Trading Terms
Short definitions for reading scanner cards.
Entry
The planned price where the trade starts.
Stop
The price where the trade idea is wrong.
Target
The first planned profit area.
R:R
Reward compared with risk. 2.0 means aiming for twice the risk.
Call
An option that can gain when price rises.
Put
An option that can gain when price falls.
Contract
The option being traded.
Strike
The option's selected price level.
Expiration
The date the option expires.
Contract Cost
The estimated total cost to buy one option contract — one contract usually represents 100 shares, so this is the full price, not a per-share price.
Best Quality
Kairos' top contract choice because it should be the easiest and most reliable to trade.
Balanced
A lower-cost choice that still keeps solid trade quality.
Budget
The lowest-cost choice that still meets Kairos' minimum quality bar, but it may be less ideal than the other choices.
Breakeven
A stop moved to entry so the trade risk is reduced.
Watchlist
A short list of tickers to keep checking.
Position
A trade that is currently open.
Ready
The setup has enough evidence to review for entry.
Wait
The setup is developing, but not ready yet.
Skip
The scanner did not rank this as a current setup.
Too Late
Price has moved too far from the planned entry.
